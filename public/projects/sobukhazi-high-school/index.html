About & Heritage

From dumpsite to
district champions.

Built on what was once a dumpsite in Mzilikazi, close to Mzilikazi High School — just as Sobukhazi Masuku was close to King Mzilikazi KaMatshobana, founder of Ndebele State.

Those raided and captured were cleansed by Sobukhazi Masuku — bakhazimula — and became part of the state. That is our ethos: cleansing, becoming, belonging.
