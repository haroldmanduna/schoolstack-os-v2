Heritage — Sobukhazi Masuku

From dumpsite to
district champions.

In 1970, Bulawayo needed F2 schools for students who would use their hands. Sobukhazi was built on what was once a dumpsite in Mzilikazi, close to Mzilikazi High — just as Sobukhazi Masuku was close to King Mzilikazi.

Sobukhazi Masuku, KaPhanyane OkaNqamakazi, cleansed those defeated and captured — bakhazimula — and they became Ndebele. That cleansing, that becoming, is our ethos.

Why it matters for website
Most people wrongly pronounce Sobukazi. We teach correct history, we own heritage. Sensational website will have heritage page, timeline, and correct pronunciation audio — builds pride and SEO for "Sobukhazi history".
