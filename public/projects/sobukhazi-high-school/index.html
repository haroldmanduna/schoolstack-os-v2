Heritage

From dumpsite to
district champions.

Built on what was once a dumpsite in Mzilikazi, close to Mzilikazi High School — just as Sobukhazi Masuku was close to King Mzilikazi KaMatshobana, founder of the Ndebele State.

Those raided and captured were cleansed by Sobukhazi Masuku — bakhazimula — and became part of the state. That ethos lives today: growth, unity, becoming.

F2 technical + academic. Carpentry built 90 desks and chairs for the school.
